## 📖 Quranic Guidance System


## 🤲 Dhikr (Remembrance) System

### What is Dhikr?

Dhikr (ذِكْر) means "remembrance" in Arabic. It's the Islamic practice of remembering Allah through specific phrases, prayers, or verses. Regular dhikr brings peace to the heart and strengthens faith.

### HaramBlur's Dhikr Features

**Scheduled Reminders:**
- **Fajr (Dawn)**: Morning dhikr to start your day with remembrance
- **Dhuhr (Noon)**: Midday spiritual refreshment
- **Asr (Afternoon)**: Afternoon remembrance and gratitude
- **Maghrib (Evening)**: Evening reflection and thanks
- **Isha (Night)**: Night-time contemplation and peace

**Beautiful Display:**
- **Arabic Text**: Authentic Arabic calligraphy
- **Transliteration**: Phonetic guide for pronunciation
- **Translation**: Meaning in English/French
- **Audio Option**: Listen to proper pronunciation (future feature)

### Common Dhikr Phrases in HaramBlur

**Subhanallah (سُبْحَانَ الله)** - "Glory be to Allah"
- Meaning: Expressing awe and glory of Allah
- When: Throughout the day, especially when seeing beauty

**Alhamdulillah (ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّٰهِ)** - "All praise is due to Allah"
- Meaning: Gratitude for Allah's blessings
- When: After meals, when receiving good news

**Allahu Akbar (الله أكبر)** - "Allah is the Greatest"
- Meaning: Affirming Allah's supreme greatness
- When: Moments of joy, difficulty, or reflection

**La ilaha illallah (لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ)** - "There is no god but Allah"
- Meaning: The fundamental declaration of Islamic faith
- When: Times of trial or spiritual reflection

### Customizing Dhikr

**Personalization Options:**
- **Frequency**: How often you want reminders
- **Display Style**: Overlay, notification, or both
- **Language Preference**: Arabic only, with translation, or transliteration
- **Time Windows**: Choose which prayer times for reminders
- **Duration**: How long each dhikr displays

## 🕌 Prayer Times Integration

### Accurate Prayer Calculations

HaramBlur integrates with Aladhan API to provide accurate prayer times based on your location.

**Supported Calculation Methods:**
- **Muslim World League** (Default - most widely used)
- **Islamic Society of North America (ISNA)**
- **Egyptian General Authority of Survey**
- **Umm Al-Qura University, Makkah**
- **University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi**

### Prayer Time Features

**Location-Based Accuracy:**
- **GPS Integration**: Automatic location detection
- **Manual Override**: Set custom location if needed
- **Time Zone Aware**: Adjusts for daylight saving time
- **High Precision**: Accurate to the minute

**Smart Notifications:**
- **Advance Alerts**: Notify 15 minutes before prayer time
- **Quiet Hours**: Respect quiet times (e.g., during Fajr)
- **Customizable**: Choose which prayers to be notified about
- **Gentle Reminders**: Non-intrusive notifications

### Prayer Tracking

**Daily Prayer Support:**
- **Prayer Times Display**: Always visible in the app
- **Next Prayer Indicator**: Shows time until next prayer
- **Prayer History**: Track your prayer consistency
- **Qibla Direction**: Compass feature for prayer direction
