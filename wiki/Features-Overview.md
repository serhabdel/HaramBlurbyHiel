## 👁️ Smart Content Protection

### Face Detection & Blurring
HaramBlur uses advanced AI to automatically detect and blur human faces in photos, videos, and apps.

**What it does:**
- **Real-time Detection**: Scans your screen continuously as you browse
- **Precise Blurring**: Applies gray rectangles exactly over detected faces
- **Multi-face Support**: Handles multiple faces in the same image
- **Cross-app Protection**: Works in browsers, social media, galleries, and more

**Why it matters:**
> *"The eyes are the window to the soul. HaramBlur helps you maintain your gaze and protect your heart from inappropriate visual content."*

### Intelligent Content Analysis
Beyond just faces, HaramBlur analyzes content to identify potentially inappropriate material.

**Smart Detection:**
- **NSFW Content**: AI-powered detection of inappropriate images and videos
- **Gender Classification**: Helps with appropriate content filtering
- **Context Awareness**: Understands different types of content and situations
- **Adaptive Learning**: Improves detection based on your preferences

**Real-world examples:**
- Blurs faces in social media photos
- Protects you while browsing websites
- Works in messaging apps and galleries
- Functions across all Android applications

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### On-Device Processing
Your privacy is our top priority - everything happens on your phone.

**Privacy Protection:**
- **Zero Data Upload**: Nothing leaves your device
- **Local AI Processing**: All analysis happens locally
- **No Cloud Dependencies**: Works without internet for core features
- **Secure Storage**: Settings and data stay on your device

**What this means:**
- Your photos and browsing history stay private
- No third-party access to your content
- Works offline for complete privacy

## 🔧 Customization Options

### Detection Sensitivity
Adjust HaramBlur to match your comfort level and needs.

**Sensitivity Levels:**
- **Conservative**: More aggressive filtering, fewer false positives

**Whitelist Options:**
