## 📧 Ways to Get Help

### Primary Support Channels

- **Best for**: General questions, sharing experiences
- **Response time**: Community-driven (varies)

**3. Documentation (This Wiki)**

## 💬 Community Guidelines

### Code of Conduct

**Our Community Standards:**
- **Respectful**: Treat all members with respect and kindness
- **Inclusive**: Welcome users from all backgrounds and experiences
- **Helpful**: Share knowledge and assist others when possible
- **Constructive**: Provide feedback that's helpful and actionable
- **Islamic Values**: Maintain decorum appropriate to Islamic principles

### Discussion Guidelines

**Posting Effectively:**
- **Be specific**: Include device model, Android version, app version
- **Use search**: Check if your question has been asked before
- **Stay on topic**: Keep discussions relevant to HaramBlur
- **Be patient**: Allow time for community responses
- **Follow up**: Update your posts with solutions you find

**What to Include in Posts:**
```
📱 Device: Samsung Galaxy S21
🤖 Android: Version 12
📦 App Version: 1.0.0
❓ Problem: [Clear description]
🔍 What I've tried: [Steps already taken]
```

### Content Policies

**Appropriate Content:**
- ✅ **Technical discussions** about HaramBlur
- ✅ **User experience sharing**
- ✅ **Feature suggestions** and improvements
- ✅ **Bug reports** with details
- ✅ **Helpful tutorials** and guides

**Inappropriate Content:**
- ❌ **Haram content** or inappropriate material
- ❌ **Spam** or irrelevant posts
- ❌ **Harassment** or disrespectful behavior
- ❌ **Commercial promotion** without permission
- ❌ **Personal attacks** or arguments

## 🐛 Bug Reports

### How to Report Bugs

**Step 1: Check Existing Issues**
- Search [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/serhabdel/HaramBlur/issues)
- See if your bug has been reported
- Add details to existing reports instead of creating duplicates

**Step 2: Gather Information**
```markdown
**Bug Report Template:**
- Device: [e.g., Samsung Galaxy S21]
- Android Version: [e.g., Android 12]
- HaramBlur Version: [e.g., 1.0.0]
- Steps to reproduce: [Detailed steps]
- Expected behavior: [What should happen]
- Actual behavior: [What actually happens]
- Logs: [Error messages, if any]
- Screenshots: [If applicable]
```

**Step 3: Submit Report**
- Go to [Issues](https://github.com/serhabdel/HaramBlur/issues)
- Click "New Issue"
- Choose "Bug Report" template
- Fill in all details
- Submit

### Bug Report Quality

**High-Quality Reports:**
- **Reproducible**: Clear steps to reproduce the issue
- **Specific**: Exact error messages and conditions
- **Complete**: All required information included
- **Recent**: Tested with latest app version

**Low-Quality Reports:**
- ❌ "App crashes" (no details)
- ❌ "Doesn't work" (insufficient information)
- ❌ "Fix it" (no reproduction steps)

## 🚀 Feature Requests

### Suggest New Features

**Types of Requests:**
- **Detection Improvements**: Better content recognition
- **Islamic Features**: New spiritual features
- **Performance Enhancements**: Speed and battery improvements
- **User Interface**: Better design and usability
- **Integration**: Support for other apps/services

### Submit Feature Requests

**Request Template:**
```markdown
**Feature Request:**
- Title: [Clear, descriptive title]
- Problem: [What's the problem this solves?]
- Solution: [Describe your proposed solution]
- Benefits: [Who benefits and how?]
- Alternatives: [Other solutions considered?]
- Additional context: [Screenshots, examples, etc.]
```

### Feature Request Process

**What Happens Next:**
1. **Review**: Team reviews your request
2. **Discussion**: Community discusses feasibility
3. **Prioritization**: Added to development roadmap
4. **Implementation**: Development begins if approved
5. **Testing**: Beta testing with community
6. **Release**: Feature released to all users

### Vote on Features

- **👍 Upvote**: Show support for features you want
