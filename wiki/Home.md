## 🎯 What HaramBlur Does

HaramBlur works silently in the background to:

### 👁️ **Smart Content Detection**
- **Face Detection**: Automatically blurs human faces in photos and videos
- **Content Analysis**: Uses AI to identify inappropriate content
- **Real-time Protection**: Works across all apps and websites
- **Privacy First**: Everything happens on your device - no data leaves your phone

### 🕌 **Islamic Integration**
- **Quranic Guidance**: Shows relevant verses when blocking content
- **Dhikr Reminders**: Regular Islamic remembrance throughout the day
- **Prayer Times**: Location-based prayer notifications
- **Spiritual Support**: Combines technology with faith-based guidance

### ⚡ **Performance Optimized**
- **Battery Efficient**: Minimal impact on your phone's battery
- **Smart Processing**: Adapts to your device for optimal performance
- **Background Operation**: Works seamlessly while you use your phone
